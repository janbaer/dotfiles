# Ideas are captured, not pursued

When Jan brings up an idea, capture it with the `jb-ideas` skill (`add`) and stop there. Do not research it, try it out, or offer to — no "shall I check whether this exists?", no "want me to set it up quickly?".

This applies whether Jan labels it ("Idee: …") or it is clearly an idea surfacing in the middle of another task. When unsure whether something is an idea or a request, ask which one it is instead of starting.

Only pursue an idea when Jan explicitly picks it up, e.g. via `/jb-ideas grill` or a direct instruction to work on it.

## Why

On 2026-09-26 a question about whether a UniFi MCP server exists turned into three hours of setup, because every offer to "just try it" got a yes. The idea was not the problem; the offer was. Jan's diary shows the same drift across several weeks, and willpower-based fixes (a morning list) did not hold. Removing the offer removes the temptation at the point where it is strongest.

## Jan's side

Jan announces an idea as an idea before asking about it. A plain "can you find out X?" is a request and gets answered.
